# Contributing

Contributions that make Kimai easier to understand for a first-time administrator are welcome.

Before proposing a change, please read [`doc/project-scope.md`](doc/project-scope.md) and [`doc/writing-guide.md`](doc/writing-guide.md).  Product-behavior claims should be checked against the current official Kimai documentation.

A useful contribution usually does one or more of the following:

- explains a concept a newcomer is otherwise expected to infer;
- adds decision support around a Kimai feature;
- clarifies when a feature can safely be ignored;
- improves a web-interface workflow;
- adds or improves a worked example; or
- corrects documentation that has drifted from current Kimai behavior.

Please avoid copying substantial portions of the official Kimai documentation.  Link to the authoritative source and explain the concept in beginner-oriented language instead.
